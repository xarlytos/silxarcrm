## THE CONVERSATION STARTER

**Customer:** "Why should I use your platform instead of [competitor]?"

**You (in 30 seconds):**
```
"Three things:

(1) WE'RE SPECIALIZED: Built specifically for [NICHE].
    Not generic like Bland. Not developer-first like Retell.
    We know [vet clinic pain] because we've solved it 50x.

(2) WE'RE HUMANIZED: -70% of clients detect 'this is AI'.
    Not LLM magic. Real algorithms. 30+ tested.
    You sound like a person. They close better.

(3) WE SELF-IMPROVE: Every night, we analyze calls.
    Detect patterns. Update scripts. A/B test. Deploy better.
    Your system gets smarter while you sleep."
```

## COMPETITIVE RESPONSES

### **WHEN THEY SAY: "Why not Bland AI?"**

**Their Positioning:**
- "We're cheaper + Spanish + global"
- "We have 20+ languages + 100+ templates"
- "We're proven (X customers)"

**Your Response:**
```
"Bland is like buying a generic tool at Home Depot.
It works. It's cheap. But it's not built for your veterinary clinic.

We're built specifically for veterinarians:
• Our scripts know your pain: no-shows, cancellations, price objections
• Our niche knowledge: typical loss = €500/client × 3/month = €1,500/month loss
• Your ROI: Close 2-3 extra clients = €1,000+ revenue/month

Bland's generic script sounds like a robot calling a plumber.
Ours sounds like your clinic manager.

Cost difference: What's 1 extra client/month worth to you?"
```

**Follow-up if they push:**
```
"I respect that you're evaluating options. Let's run a test:
• Your top 10 prospects with Bland
• Your top 10 prospects with us
• Same week, measure which closes more

If Bland wins, you take Bland. If we win, you take us.
Neither of us wants you paying for something that doesn't work."
```

## PRICING POSITIONING

### **When They Push on Price:**

**Option 1 (Volume Play):**
```
"Our average customer: €40k/year
Your current prospecting team: 1 rep @ €30-40k/year

Our system = that 1 rep, but 3x better and never sleeps.
So you don't add a rep, we replace them.
Cost to you: €40k. Savings: €30-40k (offset).
Upside: 30-50% more calls closed.

That's profitable from day 1."
```

**Option 2 (Comparison Play):**
```
"Price comparison:
  • Bland AI: €50k (generic, slower, learning loop not clear)
  • Retell AI: €0.15/min × 200 calls/month = €300/month
    (but add dev cost + setup = €1-2k upfront)
  • 11x: €200k (enterprise, overkill)
  • Gong: €40k (post-call only, not calling)
  • Outreach: €50-100k (email+SMS focused)
  • Us: €40k (calling-first, humanized, learning loop, niche)

Your money buys more with us."
```

**Option 3 (ROI Play):**
```
"Don't think price. Think ROI.

Scenario:
  • 500 calls/month
  • 1 extra close = €2,000 revenue
  • Our system = 3-5 extra closes/month average
  • 5 closes × €2,000 = €10,000 extra revenue
  • You spend €40k, make €120k extra = €80k net profit

That's 200% ROI in year 1.

What's the ROI of a tool that doesn't close extra deals?
Doesn't matter how cheap it is."
```

## SALES CLOSE PLAYBOOK

### **30-Minute Discovery Call**

```
Min 0-5:     Rapport + agenda
             "We're here to see if calling AI makes sense for you"

Min 5-10:    Pain discovery
             "What's your biggest challenge in prospecting right now?
              • Time? (too many manual dials)
              • Conversion? (too many rejections)
              • Consistency? (good reps; bad reps)"

Min 10-15:   Solution fit
             "For [PAIN], here's what we do..."
             [Show 3-minute demo or deck]

Min 15-20:   Proof (social)
             "Here's a similar customer [NICHE]
              They went from X to Y"

Min 20-25:   Objection handling
             "Questions? Concerns? Thoughts?"

Min 25-30:   Close
             "Does this feel like it could work?
              Great. Let's get you started."
```

### **The Ask**

**Do NOT ask:** "Does this interest you?"  
(They'll say "maybe" and you lose them)

**DO ask:** "When would you want to run a test?"  
(Forces decision)

**Script:**
```
"You're looking for [SOLUTION].
We have that.
Other [NICHE] customers are seeing [PROOF].

Let's get you started this week.
Two options:

Option A: Full implementation (€40k/year, 2-week ramp)
Option B: 30-day test (€2k, see if it works)

Which makes more sense?"
```

**If they hesitate:**
```
"I sense some hesitation. What's holding you back?
[Listen]

Let me address that real quick..."
```

## THINGS TO NEVER SAY

```
❌ "We're cheaper than Bland AI"
   (Too defensive; focus on value, not price)

❌ "Retell is for developers only"
   (Could offend their dev team if they have one)

❌ "11x is overkill"
   (If they're thinking 11x, don't dismiss; show why you're better for SMB)

❌ "Gong doesn't close deals"
   (Gong is great; we're complementary, not competitive)

❌ "We're the best"
   (Arrogant; let proof points speak)

✅ Instead:
   • "We're specialized for [NICHE]"
   • "Our humanization is scientifically tested"
   • "Here's what similar customers see for ROI"
   • "Let's run a test and measure together"
```
