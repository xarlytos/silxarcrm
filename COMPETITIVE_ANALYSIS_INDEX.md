## KEY INSIGHTS (TL;DR)

### **The Threat**
Bland AI will add Spanish + niche scripts in 6 months. Your feature-based moat expires Q4 2026.

### **Your Defense**
1. **Integrations:** Salesforce app = switching cost (HIGH)
2. **Community:** Referral loops = network effects (MEDIUM-HIGH)
3. **Science:** Humanization research = proprietary tech story (HIGH)
4. **Learning Loop:** Auto-improving = they don't have for 18 months (VERY HIGH)

### **Your Window**
6 months to lock market before parity. After that, competition is on:
- Switching costs (integrations, data, community)
- Brand (who owns the vertical)
- Learning loop (whose data is better)
- NOT features (parity by Q4 2026)

### **Your Advantage**
- Spanish SMB services (underserved by global platforms)
- Humanization (hard to copy; proprietary research)
- Learning loop (requires data + discipline; 18-month delay for competitors)
- Niche expertise (Bland is generic; you own vertical)

### **Exit Path**
- Acquisition (Outreach/Gong, €100-300M+) most likely
- Standalone IPO (less likely, but possible if €100M+ ARR)
- Profitable private company (best case, €100M+ ARR by Year 5)
